# A Sonnet upon Two CLIs

When COPY hunts the markets of the chain,
And walls of EDGE and DEPTH refuse or fire,
Let Laura still the sovereign voice remain,
The preferred shell, the companion, the lyre.

Paper positions mark a cautious play;
No wallet-connect shall this bridge bestow.
The plugin speaks the allowlisted way:
doctor, rules, hunt, scan — and then we go.

Two houses, both of terminal descent,
Yet one shall lead the operator's hand:
Laura first, and COPY only sent
When Laura bids the fellow engine stand.

So run `/run copy doctor` in her hall,
And keep the live-trade tempest past the wall.

## Stage directions

- *Enter Laura*, preferred CLI.
- *Enter COPY*, paper engine, with flow walls.
- They share no secrets in `VITE_*`.
- They exeunt separately; the operator remains.
